LOOP CLUB

A fictional academic project focused on the creation of a contemporary streetwear brand and its visual identity and digital experience.

Inspired by music, skateboarding, photography and digital culture, LOOP explores the concepts of movement and repetition through a vibrant visual system and an editorial website combining e-commerce, lookbook, journal, radio and storytelling.
